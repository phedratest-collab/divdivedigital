Common questions

What is the difference between GMB and Google Business Profile?

They are the same thing. Google renamed Google My Business to Google Business Profile, but many people still call it GMB.

How fast can map rankings improve?

Practices with clear gaps, like wrong categories or missing services, often see movement within 4 to 8 weeks of fixes.

Will you post fake reviews?

Never. Fake reviews break Google's rules and can get your profile suspended. We help you ask real patients at the right moment, which works better anyway.

Do you need ownership of our profile?

No. We request manager access. Your practice always stays the primary owner.
