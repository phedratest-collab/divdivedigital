Common questions

How long does a new orthodontic website take?

Most practice websites take 4 to 6 weeks from kickoff to launch, depending on how quickly content and feedback come back.

Will we lose our Google rankings?

Not when it is done right. We map every old URL to its new page, keep what is working and monitor rankings closely after launch.

Can we keep our current domain and branding?

Yes. We can work with your existing brand or refresh it as part of the project.

Is the contact form HIPAA safe?

Our consult forms only collect contact details and the treatment of interest. If you need intake forms with health information, we connect a HIPAA compliant form provider.
